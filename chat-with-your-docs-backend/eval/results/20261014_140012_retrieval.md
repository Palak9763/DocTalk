- **`+ expand`**: + Multi-perspective query expansion (2 diverse variants)
- **`+ hyde`**: + Hypothetical document embeddings (zero-shot alignment passage)
- **`full_selfrag`**: + Self-RAG adaptive routing, chunk grading, and citation verification
